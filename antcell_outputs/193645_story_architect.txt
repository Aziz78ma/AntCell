**Project 1: The Enchanted Forest**

To develop the script and storyboard for "The Enchanted Forest," I'll focus on Rosie's journey as she discovers the ancient magic in the forest. Here's a refined outline:

Act 1 (0-10 seconds):

* Introduce Rosie, a curious and playful rabbit who loves exploring the forest.
* Show Rosie stumbling upon an ancient tree with intricate carvings that seem to shimmer and glow.

Act 2 (10-20 seconds):

* The magic of the tree begins to awaken, causing the trees around Rosie to come to life.
* Rosie is initially frightened but soon becomes enchanted by the magical world unfolding before her eyes.
* As she explores this new world, Rosie realizes the potential consequences of meddling with ancient magic.

Act 3 (20-30 seconds):

* Rosie's curiosity turns to concern as she understands that her actions may have unintended effects on the forest and its inhabitants.
* The animation concludes with Rosie making a decision that showcases her growth and newfound understanding of the enchanted world.
