**Project: "Panda's Awakening"**

**Task:** Create a 30-second 3D animated short in Ghibli style about a curious panda discovering AI for the first time.

**Deliverables:**

1. A polished, 30-second 3D animated short that showcases Ling's discovery of AI.
2. A detailed breakdown of the animation process, including software used and techniques employed.
3. A comprehensive review of the team's performance and areas for improvement.

**Key Focus Areas:**

1. **Emotional Storytelling**: Ensure that the animation effectively conveys Ling's curiosity, wonder, and eventual understanding of AI.
2. **Visual Beauty**: Maintain a consistent Ghibli-inspired art style throughout the animation, blending traditional techniques with digital media.
3. **Technical Feasibility**: Utilize software such as Blender or Unreal Engine to create high-quality 3D assets and animations that can be easily exported for local video generation.

**Delegate Tasks:**

1. Story Architect: Refine the script and storyboard to ensure a clear narrative arc and emotional resonance.
2. 3D Asset Creator: Develop high-quality 3D models, textures, and rigging systems for the panda character and environment.
3. Animation Team: Bring the animation to life using the refined script, assets, and techniques.

**Critical Review Points:**

1. Ensure consistency with previous projects in terms of art style, color palette, and texture.
2. Pay attention to lighting, camera angles, and composition to create a visually stunning animation.
3. Review the animation for pacing, timing, and emotional impact.

**Project Timeline:**

* Day 1-3: Refine script and storyboard
* Day 4-6: Develop 3D assets and rigging systems
* Day 7-10: Animation production and post-production
