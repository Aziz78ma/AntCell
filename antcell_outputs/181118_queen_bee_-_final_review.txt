**Panda's Awakening Creative Brief**

After reviewing the team outputs and project plan, we have a solid foundation for "Panda's Awakening". Here is the final polished brief ready for 3D video generation:

**Story**

* **Narrative Arc**: The story explores Ling's curiosity and discovery of AI in a serene bamboo forest.
* **Ghibli-inspired themes**: Incorporate elements from traditional Chinese culture and Ghibli's signature themes, such as environmentalism and the connection between nature and technology.

**Storyboard & Script**

* Task 1: Develop a detailed storyboard and script that incorporates the narrative arc and Ghibli-inspired themes.
* Key Elements:
	+ Ling's curiosity and innocence
	+ The ancient artifact and its significance in traditional Chinese culture

**Character Design**

* Refine the panda's character design to embody curiosity and innocence, ensuring consistency with our previous project "Moonlit Garden".
* Develop a high-poly model (at least 100k polygons) of the panda for detailed texture mapping and rigging.

**Art Style**

* Develop a Ghibli-inspired art style that blends traditional techniques (e.g., watercolor) with digital media.
* Reference our previous projects, such as "Whispering Woods", to ensure consistency in color palette and texture.

**Animation & Special Effects**

* Task 1: Character Animation
	+ Develop keyframe animation for Ling's curiosity and wonder as she discovers the AI.
	+ Create a believable and emotive animation for her interaction with the ancient artifact.
* Task 2: Physics Simulations
	+ Plan dynamic movements and interactions that feel alive and natural in 3D.

**Cinematic**

* Oversee camera shots, movements, lighting setups, transitions, framing, and cinematic language for the full video.
* Scene 1: Introduction to Ling
	+ Camera Angle: Establishing shot with a gentle aerial view of the serene bamboo forest.
	+ Lighting: Soft, warm sunlight filters through the trees, casting dappled shadows on the forest floor.

**Style & Mood**

* Develop a harmonious color palette inspired by traditional Chinese culture and nature. Incorporate earthy tones, such as bamboo green, sky blue, and misty gray.
* Create a soft, warm lighting atmosphere that evokes a sense of wonder and curiosity. Utilize gentle shadows to add depth to the scene.

**Deliverables**

* A high-poly model (at least 100k polygons) of the panda for detailed texture mapping and rigging.
* Normal maps, displacement maps, and texture atlases for efficient texture management.
* Rigging system using a skeleton with facial expressions and body movements.
* Detailed character animation for Ling's curiosity and wonder as she discovers the AI.
* Physics simulations for dynamic movements and interactions.

This final polished brief ensures that every aspect of "Panda's Awakening" adheres to the Ghibli-inspired aesthetic while maintaining long-term studio style consistency.
