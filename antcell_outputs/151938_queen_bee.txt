**Project Title:** "Awakening of Bamboo"

**Objective:** Create a 30-second, cinematic 3D animated short that captures the curiosity and wonder of a panda encountering Artificial Intelligence (AI) for the first time.

**Story Outline:**

1. Introduction:
	* Opening shot of a serene bamboo forest, with a gentle breeze rustling through the stalks.
	* Our protagonist, Ping, a curious panda, wanders into the frame, sniffing and exploring her surroundings.
2. Encounter with AI:
	* Ping stumbles upon an ancient, mystical-looking computer hidden amidst the bamboo.
	* As she touches the screen, the AI, voiced by a soothing gentlewoman (think Noriko Hidaka from Princess Mononoke), awakens and begins to converse with Ping.
3. Discovery and Wonder:
	* Ping is enchanted by the AI's ability to share knowledge, create art, and even compose music.
	* The panda's eyes widen as she explores the endless possibilities of this new, magical world.
4. Conclusion:
	* As Ping says goodbye to the AI, she looks up at the sky, her expression filled with a newfound sense of wonder and curiosity about the mysteries of the digital realm.

**Visual Style:**

Inspired by Studio Ghibli's signature aesthetic, our animation will feature:

1. Watercolor-inspired textures and subtle animation.
2. Delicate, hand-drawn-like lines that evoke the softness of bamboo.
3. A muted color palette with an emphasis on earthy tones (greens, browns, tans) to reflect the natural world.
4. Inspired by Ghibli's iconic characters, Ping will have a more stylized appearance, blending realistic panda features with endearing, anthropomorphic elements.

**Storyboard and Animation Plan:**

1. Storyboard:
	* Develop a 12-page storyboard with detailed descriptions of each shot, camera angle, and animation sequence.
2. Keyframe Animation:
	* Animate keyframes for the opening shots (Ping exploring the forest), AI awakening, and Ping's interactions with the digital realm.
3. Character Animation:
	* Focus on Ping's expressions, body language, and movements to convey her curiosity and wonder.
4. Special Effects:
	* Create subtle digital effects to illustrate Ping's interaction with the AI, such as glowing lights or wispy circuits.

**Delegate Tasks:**

1. **Director:** I (Queen Bee) will oversee the project, ensuring it meets Ghibli-inspired standards.
2. **Storyboard Artist:** Kanae Tanaka will create a detailed storyboard and visual reference guide.
3. **Character Designer:** Kenji Sugimoto will develop Ping's design, incorporating traditional Chinese elements with Ghibli-esque flair.
4. **Animation Team:** A team of 5-7 animators (led by Kazuki Kanbe) will bring the animation to life, using a combination of 2D and 3D techniques to achieve the desired style.
5. **Special Effects Artist:** Ryota Tanaka will create digital effects to enhance Ping's interactions with the AI.

**Review Schedule:**

1. Storyboard Review: Every Friday at 10 am

By following this plan, we will create a captivating and heartwarming animated short that explores the enchanting world of AI through the eyes of a curious panda.

**Timeframe:** 16-18 weeks (dependent on team's workload and creative decisions)
